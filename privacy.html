Maas Media Channels

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

This policy explains how Maas Media Channels, an internal tool operated by MAAS MEDIA, accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Data we access

With the explicit authorization of the person who manages a YouTube channel, the app accesses, read-only:

  • through the YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly): the authorized channel's ID and name, and the list of its uploaded videos with title, publish date, duration and description chapters;
  • through the YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API (yt-analytics.readonly): aggregated statistics of the channel's own videos, namely audience retention curves, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate.

These are aggregated channel statistics. The app does not receive any personal data about viewers, and it does not access the user's email, contacts or any other Google service.

How we use it

Only to produce internal reports on how MAAS MEDIA's videos perform, so the team can improve future videos. The app never uploads, edits or deletes content on YouTube.

How we share it

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Reports are read only by MAAS MEDIA staff.

Maas Media Channels' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the computer of the MAAS MEDIA staff member running the tool, in files readable only by that user. They are not uploaded to any server. Statistics are kept as long as they are useful for the weekly analysis; on request we delete all data related to a channel within 30 days.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to the address below.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
