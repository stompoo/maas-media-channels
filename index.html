Maas Media Channels

Maas Media Channels is an internal analytics tool built and operated by MAAS MEDIA, an Italian YouTube-first media company. It helps the people who run MAAS MEDIA's YouTube channels understand where viewers stop watching each video, so the next videos can be written and edited better.

What the app does

  1. The manager of a YouTube channel signs in with Google and authorizes read-only access to that channel.
  2. Once a week the tool lists the channel's new long-form videos and reads each video's audience retention curve and reach statistics.
  3. It matches the retention curve with the video's public captions and writes a short internal report: the moments where viewers drop off, the parts that hold their attention, and the moments viewers rewatch.

The app is read-only: it never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube. It is not a public service and is used only by MAAS MEDIA staff for the channels they manage.

Google data it uses

  • youtube.readonly: to identify the authorized channel and list its uploaded videos (title, publish date, duration, chapters).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's own audience retention curves (YouTube Analytics API) and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate (YouTube Reporting API).

All data stays on the computer of the person running the tool. See the privacy policy.
